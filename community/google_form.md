# Submit a case or correction using Google Forms

[Open the public submission form](https://forms.gle/qLpe7RqhSpFp7awy5)

[Direct Google Forms link](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeD8wgFLzgAZImz4BpFJtFj6CLe_S1o7L1VIWNyrbRcb5z9TQ/viewform?usp=publish-editor)

The form accepts new cases, corrections and additional sources. No GitHub account is needed. An anonymous-access check on 27 October 2026 (New York date) returned the form successfully without login cookies.

The 12 questions cover submission type, an existing case ID where relevant, location, incident date/uncertainty, source URLs, the reported episode or correction, contemporary government and force authority where known, discovery provenance, conflicting evidence, human/AI source-reading status, optional public credit, and acknowledgement that research content may be published after review.

Email collection is set to **Do not collect**. The form has no email question and no sign-in requirement from a one-response limit. Respondents can save their response-edit link and correct their submission later. Public response summaries are disabled. The footer identifies **UMass Amherst** and provides Google's Contact form owner control. The owner's email address was absent from both visible text and returned HTML in the anonymous-access check. A signed-in viewer may see their own account address beside Google's Not shared notice.

Submissions are held in a separate response sheet for source and coding review. They do not automatically change the public CSV. Research content and optional public credit may be published after review, as stated on the form. Please do not submit private contact details, unnecessary personal identifiers or complete copyrighted article bodies. See [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for the review process.

Initial research and this form were prepared with OpenAI Codex at Aritra Basu's direction. Human/AI review labels concern the source-reading process; they do not establish the truth of an underlying police allegation.
