# Independent provisional case coding

This folder records one AI coder's source-context review of the completed 1,132-article, four-state acquisition cohort. It is case finding within a selected source frame, not a census of police practices. The acquisition cohort was selected from a larger public TOI sitemap inventory; the four-state design covers West Bengal, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana. Historical material, other-state events and actors are retained for transparent exclusions. Source article bodies remain private in the acquisition output; this folder republishes metadata and original coding only.

## Reproducible signal frame

`build_ledger.py` reads `../collection/output/article_review_cohort_private.jsonl` and preserves hand-coded event-to-article mappings. It uses `analysis_body` when available and additionally truncates at the explicit marker `We also published the following articles recently`. Generic navigation markers such as `You Can Also Check` are not used as truncation points: substantive article prose can resume after them. This is conservative, reproducible cleaning, not a guarantee that all recommendations or unrelated content have been removed.

The independent expanded frame is the union of:

1. Firearm term `guns? | rifles? | pistols? | revolvers? | firearms? | weapons? | carbines? | arms` AND taking term `snatch* | grab* | wrest* | disarm* | overpower* | seiz* | tak* | took | taken` anywhere in the cleaned article, with word boundaries and case-insensitive matching. This intentionally broad rule detects ordinary police weapon seizures and possession as false positives; actor and object direction require source review.
2. `reconstruc | re.enact | recreat` anywhere in cleaned article text.
3. The acquisition agent's `body_parade_or_stripping_flag`.

At the completed snapshot, 245 distinct articles fall in this expanded union: 131 firearm-plus-taking, 27 reconstruction, and 111 parade/stripping flags (the components overlap). All 245 received AI source-context review: 70 mapped to the event ledger and 175 received a context exclusion. Zero target-signal articles remain unread. Signal review uses article headline, relevant body contexts and substantive body passages; it is not independent human verification of the reporting or of every factual assertion in an article.

Including additional broad-event reads, article-level outputs partition all 1,132 IDs into 83 mapped reports, 183 reviewed context exclusions and 866 deterministic no-target-signal articles. The complete lexical no-signal pool has887 articles, of which21 were additionally reviewed and the remaining866 retain only the deterministic classification. The last category means that the stated lexical screen found no target signal; it is not an AI-validated negative or evidence that a target event did not occur. A separate acquisition agent is auditing a stratified random sample from the low-signal pool. Sampling results belong to that agent's audit, not this one-coder positive-case validation.

## Event interpretation

`event_ledger.csv` and `.json` contain 44 provisional rows, including background shootings, nonfatal shootings, central-force cases, private mob violence, threats, historical incidents and ordinary reconstruction. The total44 is not a substantive outcome count and must not be used as an encounter denominator. Multiple reports map to the same event, and one combined report can map to two distinct events. People and events are separate units.

The main identified outcome is a fatal OR nonfatal shooting by state police in the four states in2024–2026 with an immediate reported allegation that a suspect/group took or attempted to take an officer's firearm. Four events are identified, all fatal; no additional nonfatal qualifying event was identified in the reviewed signal frame: three West Bengal events (Baruipur, Gaighata, Shamsherganj) and one Telangana event (Nizamabad/Riyaz). Absence of additional identified events in Kerala/Tamil Nadu does not establish true zero incidence or full newspaper recall. The associated police account is an allegation, not a proven mechanism or finding of legal justification/illegality. A separate2025 West Bengal BSF group weapon-taking account is retained as a central-force sensitivity case and excluded from the state-police outcome. A2003 Zaheerabad gun-taking case is historical undivided Andhra Pradesh and excluded from current incidence.

The ledger includes machine-readable positive-label booleans: `main_reported_gun_taking_then_police_shot` (four), fatal subset (four), nonfatal subset (zero identified), broad police public parade (five), explicit reconstruction plus public parade (three), and explicit reconstruction plus shirtless police parade (one). False values indicate failure to meet the strict reported-text label, not proven real-world absence. Reconstruction-plus-parade comprises Behala, Saokat Molla and Jahangir Khan; the strict shirtless subset is Behala only.

Five explicit reported police public-parade events are identified in West Bengal: Bonny/Roy (May28 2026), Jahangir Khan (June11), Saokat Molla (July2), Tirthankar Ghosh (July18), and the Behala/Sarsuna accused (October27). This broad category includes reports framed as ordinary investigation/reconstruction; it does not itself establish humiliation or stripping. Clothing is separately coded. The October27 event has a shirtless/barefoot/waist-rope account; Bonny has a vest/shorts/waist-rope account; Jahangir has a T-shirt/shorts account; the other two have no clothing description in reviewed sources. Do not call all five naked or semi-naked. The separate October25 Behala naked parade was private mob action. The Telangana CM's stripping/parading threat is speech, not an implemented event. A Tamil Nadu road-rage stripping assault involving a serving constable is retained as private action by an officer, not an official custodial parade.

A police visit with an accused to reconstruct a crime does not automatically count as a coercive parade. A March2025 Panagarh reconstruction is retained separately; ordinary clothing and spectators alone do not establish coercion. The May28 Bonny report mentions another police visit on MondayMay25, but does not supply enough detail to establish a distinct qualifying public parade; it is logged in `event_uncertainties.csv` rather than counted as a second parade.

Dates are event dates in Indian local time when supported. Publication dates remain separate. Weekday-derived dates, historical year-only dates, report conflicts and delayed hospital deaths are explicitly marked. `event_uncertainties.csv` separates genuine source uncertainty from review incompleteness. No source-context candidates remain unread, but event uncertainty remains and the source frame has imperfect recall.

## Limits for quantitative use

These are selected-source identified event counts, suitable for a transparent research note or bounded sensitivity analysis. They do not identify a causal effect of party control, a complete incidence rate, a legal finding of extrajudicial killing, or the probability that the reported police accounts are true. Public attention, vocabulary, source availability, geography assignment, reporting density and different custody/reconstruction opportunities can change observed counts. Unknown source coverage or state-months must remain unknown rather than be filled with event zeros. A broad fatal-encounter denominator has not been completely coded and is intentionally not supplied.

Files can be regenerated by running `python3 build_ledger.py` from any directory. Raw copyrighted article bodies are not included in these output tables.

## Additional synonym sensitivity check

After freezing the primary245-article union, an independent supplementary screen searched the remaining unreviewed-status bodies for a firearm term together with `steal*|stole*|loot*|pick*|yank*|pull*|pluck*|captur*|get hold`. Seven extra reports were found and their weapon contexts reviewed. None supplied an additional officer-firearm-taking followed by police shooting claim. These seven are listed separately in `additional_synonym_sensitivity_review.csv`; they do not alter the frozen primary-union counts or main CSV status partition. This is targeted sensitivity checking, not a random recall estimate. One report is a nonfatal shooting after an alleged privately concealed gun was pulled; this illustrates why gun possession and taking an officer firearm are coded separately.
