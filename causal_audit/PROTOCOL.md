# Frozen exploratory comparison protocol

Written after the West Bengal cluster was known and before comparative case coding. This is not a preregistration.

The main candidate donor states are Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Telangana. Government histories and wider donor eligibility are being audited separately. BJP-led government, BJP cabinet participation, and electoral alliance are distinct variables.

## Measurement

Apply the same URL retrieval rules, source date window, actual article-body extraction and event deduplication across all states. The acquisition frame is the publisher's listed 2024–2026 sitemap inventory, supplemented by current feeds. It is not a census of real police incidents or all published news.

Outcomes are separate:

1. An individual officer-firearm-taking claim immediately followed by police shooting of that person. Record attempts, completed taking, and fatality separately.
2. Police-led public parades explicitly described as reconstruction. Code clothing and actor, excluding mob stripping and different jail episodes.
3. Broader reported fatal police encounters, as a secondary outcome.

Central-force and group-attempt events are sensitivity categories. Use event dates in IST. Article publication dates can corroborate or bound dates; sitemap modification dates cannot assign incident months. Retain unresolved dates and mechanisms.

## Comparison and synthetic control

West Bengal's transition is May 9, 2026. Exclude May from the main comparison; use June 1–October 27 as the 149-day post period. A same-season 2025 comparison is a sensitivity check. Kerala and Tamil Nadu also changed governments in May 2026 but remained non-BJP; record turnover separately.

Choose synthetic-control weights from pre-transition observations only. Fit nonnegative weights summing to one. Report pretreatment fit and leave-one-donor-out sensitivity. If rare or all-zero pretreatment outcomes leave weights unidentified, report the range of post predictions among equally good pretreatment fits rather than selecting a convenient weight vector.

Do not fill incomplete or unreviewed state-months with zero. A zero may be reported only within an explicitly completed retrieval-and-review frame. Measurement bounds concern that frame, not undiscovered incidents.

One treated state and a small donor pool provide weak causal inference. Treatment-label placebo ranks have coarse resolution and require exchangeability to become randomization p-values. No causal BJP effect has yet been established.
