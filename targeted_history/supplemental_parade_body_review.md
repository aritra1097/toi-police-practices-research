## Files

`supplemental_parade_body_review.csv` retains the complete staged queue, including awaiting/unread rows. `supplemental_parade_events.csv` contains only newly promoted episodes with the shared35-column event-table schema. `supplemental_parade_case_events.csv` also retains reconfirmed known episodes for source joins; do not count these duplicates as new. Neither is a census. `supplemental_parade_review_decisions.json` records manual judgements, and the rendering script regenerates this snapshot as acquisition continues.

The primary exact target is an underclothed/naked public police parade explicitly linked to a case-specific scene reconstruction. Ordinary escorts, court transport, private mobs, prison discipline, foreign episodes, artistic reenactments and generic investigation are separate categories.
