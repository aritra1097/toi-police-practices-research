Government labels describe the administration then in office and do not establish political direction or comparative incidence.

Key corrections: Ranbir’s 3 July 2009 killing occurred under Nishank, sworn in 27 June, rather than Khanduri. Karnataka 2001 is Krishna’s INC administration despite his later BJP affiliation. Telangana 2015/2019 party name is TRS, rather than its later BRS name. Bihar October 2023 is Nitish JD(U) with RJD/Congress Mahagathbandhan, before the January 2024 NDA return. NIMHANS is a national institute, but the reported shooting team was Karnataka state Garuda commandos. Mehran’s court visit was in Uttarakhand; his escort was Shahjahanpur UP police.

The JSON preserves tenure sources separately from party sources and retains source-quality limits. Most tenure checks use official CM/assembly/government pages. Krishna’s party additionally uses a mirrored Government of Karnataka handbook, with host status disclosed; Nishank’s party and some coalition detail use contemporary primary reporting. Telangana’s second oath uses primary party history alongside official election evidence. No exhaustive day-specific cabinet or legislative-support roster is claimed. In particular, Bihar’s initial 2022 HAM cabinet inclusion is not silently carried into October 2023.

## Source register

- **KA_terms**: [Karnataka Legislative Council: Chief Ministers since 1947](https://kla.kar.nic.in/council/cms.htm). official legislative history; search-index text available, direct open timed out. S. M. Krishna 11 October 1999–28 May 2004; Siddaramaiah 13 May 2013–15 May 2018.
- **KA_party2001**: [Government of Karnataka, A Handbook of Karnataka (2015), p.125](https://www.nammakpsc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/A-Handbook-of-Karnataka-2015.pdf). primary government publication hosted on a third-party mirror; original government URL not recovered in bounded search. Chief-minister chronology explicitly lists S. M. Krishna and Siddaramaiah as INC; primary-author attribution rather than treating mirror as an official host.
- **KA_party2013**: [Karnataka Legislative Assembly review 2013–18, Appendix II](https://kla.kar.nic.in/assembly/research/la_review_2013-18.pdf). official legislative publication reproducing governor notification. 10 May 2013 appointment notification says Siddaramaiah was elected leader of Karnataka Congress Legislature Party. Appointment notification date differs from 13 May assumption of office.
- **UK_terms**: [Uttarakhand Chief Minister Office: Former Chief Ministers and their tenure](https://cm.uk.gov.in/former-chief-ministers/). official CM history. Nishank tenure begins 27 June 2009; predecessor Khanduri tenure ends 26 June. Ranbir killing on 3 July belongs to Nishank administration.
- **UK_party**: [PTI/TOI, Pokhriyal to be new Uttarakhand CM, 24 June 2009](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/pokhriyal-to-be-new-uttarakhand-cm/articleshow/4697118.cms). contemporaneous primary news report, supplementary to official tenure source. Reports his election as leader of BJP legislature party; announcement is not his oath date.
- **UP_terms**: [Uttar Pradesh Legislative Council: Chief Minister and tenure](https://vidhanparishadproceedings.up.gov.in/government/page/chief-minister). official legislative history. Akhilesh Yadav 15 March 2012–19 March 2017; Yogi Adityanath first term 19 March 2017–25 March 2022 and second from 25 March 2022.
- **UP_SP**: [Uttar Pradesh Legislative Assembly: former chief minister Akhilesh Yadav](https://upvidhansabhaproceedings.gov.in/en/prev_chiefminister_akhileshyadav). official biography. Records election as Samajwadi Legislature Party leader on 10 March 2012 and chief minister on 15 March.
- **UP_BJP**: [Uttar Pradesh Legislative Council: Chief Minister](https://www.vidhanparishad.up.gov.in/chief-minister). official legislative profile. Identifies Yogi Adityanath with Bharatiya Janata Party; used with historical term dates, not as sole temporal evidence.
- **TG_2014**: [Khammam district government: K. Chandrashekar Rao profile](https://khammam.telangana.gov.in/kcr-profile/). official district biography. Identifies Telangana Rashtra Samithi and his first chief-minister assumption on 2 June 2014.
- **TG_2018**: [BRS official party timeline (historical TRS entries)](https://brsonline.in/about/timeline/). primary party history, partisan source. Records second oath on 13 December 2018 and historical TRS name. Do not replace incident-date TRS with current BRS branding.

Chandrashekar Rao under Telangana Rashtra Samithi.
