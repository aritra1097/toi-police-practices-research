## Institution and election qualifications

BSF and CISF are central forces administered through [Union MHA Police-II](https://www.mha.gov.in/en/divisionofmha/police-ii-division), also identified in the [MHA annual report](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/AnnualreportEnglish_04102023.pdf). State-party labels alone do not establish who commanded a central-force incident. CRPF/state-police joint operations require separate agency coding. Dum Dum is correctional administration rather than a street-police operation.

For election duty, [section 28A](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1941/1/195143.pdf) places designated officers under ECI election supervision. The [ECI force-deployment manual](https://www.eci.gov.in/eci-backend/public/api/download?url=LMAhAK6sOPBp%2FNFF0iRfXbEB1EVSLT41NNLRjYNJJP1KivrUxbfqkDatmHy12e%2FzVx8fLfn2ReU7TfrqYobgIvZ0n19tFMJzYNUMlQdr0LR9wv8%2B5nXgMo0aziJ0WKXcD2mjzPfzEgKRfE3Jice0BpSrPmQz9vt2aDMViYDfJ9IM%2F2fqtXgeR74oE38CYbCb) explains roles shared across ECI, MHA and state/district functionaries. This 2023 manual is framework evidence, not a record of the 2021 Sitalkuchi firing order. Mamata/AITC was the state government on 10 April 2021; CISF was the reported firing force. Neither fact establishes a partisan order or causal responsibility.
