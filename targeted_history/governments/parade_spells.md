## Coding cautions

Delhi’s AAP elected government and the BJP-led Union government must occupy separate fields. MHA’s parliamentary answer explicitly places Delhi Police under the Union Ministry of Home Affairs and Lt Governor. Calling these simply “AAP police cases” would misstate institutional authority.

Punjab was an SAD-led coalition with BJP participation, not a BJP-chief-minister government. The Chinkot allegation concerns private action involving a constable on long leave; it is not an official accused reconstruction operation.

Maharashtra in September 2020 was the Uddhav Thackeray, undivided Shiv Sena-led MVA coalition with NCP and Congress. Later party splits must not change this historical label.

Rajasthan Assembly House tenure dates are not CM oath dates. The separately sourced oaths are 17 December 2018 for Gehlot and 15 December 2023 for Sharma. Punjab’s exact term boundaries were not reconstructed in this bounded search; the contemporary July 2015 official release directly identifies Badal and the SAD–BJP alliance.

- **DL_oath** — [Delhi Legislative Assembly: Chief Minister profile](https://delhiassembly.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-06/cm.pdf): Kejriwal second oath 14 February 2015 and third oath 16 February 2020; AAP founder and convener.
- **DL_police** — [MHA Lok Sabha unstarred question 3279, 6 December 2016, p.2](https://www.mha.gov.in/MHA1/Par2017/pdfs/par2016-pdfs/ls-061216/3279.pdf): Delhi Police is responsible to Parliament through the Union Ministry of Home Affairs and Lt Governor of Delhi. This is separate from the elected NCT government.
