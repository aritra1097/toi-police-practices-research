# National historical officer-firearm-taking leads

This purposive search checked TOI reporting for an allegation that an accused or prisoner took, or tried to take, an officer's firearm and was then shot by police. Twelve event leads precede 2024. They are examples for further verification, not a national count or representative sample. Police allegations remain allegations. Raw article bodies are not reproduced here. Dates below are event dates; publication dates are separately preserved in the JSON ledger.

The machine-readable scope flag selects ten ordinary-crime custody/arrest/investigation leads for the main comparison. Alair is a terror-suspect transport case and NIMHANS is a psychiatric-hospital armed standoff; both are retained separately. A group allegation is not automatically an insurgency or Maoist case. The reviewed ordinary-crime group cases include the Deepika pair, the Bareilly escort group, the Disha suspects and the Vaishali pair. Their individual gun-taking attribution remains limited as shown below.

The JSON preserves event/publication dates, displayed update timestamps, custody context, attempt versus completed allegation, named versus group attribution, outcome and uncertainty. TOI article IDs are deduplicated across ordinary and AMP URL forms. These records are provisional AI source-context coding, not human validation or findings that the shootings were lawful or unlawful. No new verified historical West Bengal case emerged from this finite national search. That is not evidence of absence.

Do not add these selected examples to only one side of the frozen four-state comparison, count all deaths as separate gun-taking events, or estimate rarity or probability from this list. A common retrieval and validation frame is still needed for comparisons.
