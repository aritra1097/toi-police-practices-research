# Targeted collection audit

Independent AI/subagent review, 27 October 2026 in the user's New York calendar. This audit used local code, queue files, saved records and raw responses only; it made no TOI requests. Collection was still running, so the counts below are snapshots, not a completion certificate.

## Material findings and release conditions

1. **Known error bodies must not count as recovered articles.** At 2026-10-28 00:23 UTC, 703 record files were present. Four first-process records remained marked `success` despite containing only `ERR-RTF`: 4489279, 5529430, 5563122 and 5725039. The on-disk collector now rejects these markers and tries HTML fallback, but the original running process loaded older code. A final cached pass with the current module must repair them or mark them unresolved. The earlier success total is not a usable-body total.

2. **HTML identity and cached provenance corrections need to run, not merely exist on disk.** The original fallback matched the page canonical ID but selected the first NewsArticle object without matching its identity. The revised code requires a unique matching Article object, or a sole object without an explicit article ID on a matching canonical page, and labels the validation method. Cached HTML records lacking that validation are recollected. The revised cache path also merges additional queue routes and preserves prior record versions. A final pass is necessary because running processes retain their loaded definitions. One earlier cached record, 37424220, lacked its new external-source route; the extended queue remains the authoritative selection record until cached records are refreshed.

3. **The source queue is purposive, not a reviewed-event denominator.** The baseline queue has 564 unique IDs and the extended queue 915 unique IDs. Supplemental rules select specific clothing/parade language, nonceremonial actor-parade language and officer-weapon wording. Unselected or URL-unresolved candidates are not negative events. Nationally discovered reports are additional selected leads and must not silently be merged into a balanced causal panel. The liveblog 72393239 is outside the articleshow-only extractor; its ordinary Disha counterpart 72400183 is queued. Do not describe all 16 national source IDs as locally recovered solely because the JSON/CSV source list exists.

4. **Dates remain bibliographic until manually coded.** Queue `source_date` is explicitly marked as dataset/archive discovery information, and external-source jobs leave it unassigned. Feed `seo.datePublished` and `dateModified` are stored separately. The collector does not infer an event date. In particular, the 2014 Ranbir report describes a 2009 killing; metadata publication must not move that event into 2014. Likewise, late-night updates, retrospective reports and weekday-derived event dates require their own provenance. The twelve national case leads are source-context web coding; raw metadata alignment was still pending for their queued source IDs at the first audit snapshot.

### National event-table interpretation

The two national event tables together contain **16 distinct source-linked reported events**, not 16 exact individual fatal gun-snatching incidents. Fourteen are within the declared ordinary-crime comparison scope; two are separately tagged sensitivity contexts (Alair terrorism-suspect transport and NIMHANS psychiatric-hospital standoff). Ten of the sixteen report fatalities and six report nonfatal shooting injuries at the article's reporting time. Of the fourteen ordinary-scope rows, eight report fatalities and six nonfatal injuries. Five rows use group-level gun-taking attribution, and three report attempted taking. These categories overlap and must not be summed as independent event types or interpreted as individual acts. Near cases, including private guard rifle theft, delayed Kasganj recapture, jail accidental-discharge allegations and crowd incidents, remain separately identified in article-review decisions/context tables. The sources establish that allegations were reported; they do not establish their truth, legality, incidence or a party effect.
