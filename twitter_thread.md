# Twitter/X thread draft

Copy each numbered paragraph into one post. This draft has not been posted to Twitter/X. The CSV is public; contributions enter through reviewed GitHub submissions.

1/10 I'm building a public dataset of reported police firearm-taking accounts and accused-person parades in India, with OpenAI Codex. The aim is to investigate historical precedents and invite source-based corrections.

2/10 Did this come from old scraped TOI files? Partly. Those files supplied historical headline candidates. We then retrieved article bodies. Targeted web searches supplied additional cases. The CSV is not just a list of direct scrape matches.

3/10 The initial CSV has 41 historical case/context entries. They include near matches and exclusions across different practices. They are NOT 41 comparable encounter killings, a complete census, or an event-rate denominator.

4/10 We separate ordinary crime, insurgency, border operations, crowds and parade allegations. For firearm-taking reports, group/individual attribution, attempted/completed taking, custody and fatal/nonfatal outcomes matter.

5/10 Earlier reported firearm-taking-to-shooting accounts occur under different governing parties. This establishes precedents. It does not show how common the practice is under each party, or establish a causal BJP effect.

6/10 Each historical entry includes the chief minister and party/coalition at the time. State incumbency is distinct from force authority: Delhi Police, BSF and CISF need Union-government context. Incumbency alone does not establish political orders.

7/10 Transparency: Codex did the initial collection, coding and analysis at my direction. Other AI agents checked parts of it. The seed rows are labelled AI-reviewed, not independently human-validated. Police claims remain attributed allegations.

8/10 Please contribute an earlier case, a correction, a conflicting source or a better government assignment. No coding needed: use the GitHub forms. Submissions are reviewed before inclusion.

10/10 The hard questions are coverage, changing news reporting, deduplication and consistent definitions. Similar wording is a lead to investigate, not proof that an encounter was staged. Help improve the evidence, including cases that challenge the hypothesis.
